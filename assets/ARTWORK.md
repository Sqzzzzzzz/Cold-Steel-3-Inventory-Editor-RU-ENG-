# Artwork

`academy_courtyard.png` is an original background generated for this editor with the built-in image generation tool. It is scenery only; every interface control is painted by Qt.

Prompt: A softly blurred, cool daylight military academy courtyard in a polished Japanese RPG environment style: European gray stone buildings and slate-roof towers, blue sky, green trees and hedges, perspective path. Wide 16:9, soft photographic defocus. No people, text, interface, icons, borders or watermark.

`hud_arrow_up.svg`, `hud_arrow_down.svg`, and `hud_check.svg` are original code-drawn control symbols. Navigation pictograms and glass frames are drawn in `qt_hud_widgets.py`. Item artwork is exclusively authentic CS3 imagery, documented in `icons/original/sources.json`; no procedural item medallions are used.
